Almost everyone thinks of a murder here. Mrs Davies comes home in the evening. Rosie and Max are lying on the living room floor. Both of them are dead. All around them: a puddle of water and broken glass. Nobody was in the house, only Oscar the cat, sitting on the sofa licking his paws. What happened here? Write your answer in the comments. [pause] Three. Two. One. [pause] The answer: Rosie and Max are two goldfish. Oscar the cat pushed the fish tank off the shelf. The glass broke, and all the water ran out.
