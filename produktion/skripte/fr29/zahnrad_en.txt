Almost everyone gets this one wrong. In Grandpa Frank's workshop, five gears are locked together. A heavy bucket hangs on a rope from the right side of the last gear. Grandpa Frank turns the first gear clockwise. Does the bucket go up or down? Write up or down in the comments. [pause] Three. Two. One. [pause] The answer: Every gear turns the opposite way to its neighbour. Gear one turns clockwise, gear two the other way, gear three clockwise again. Gear four turns the other way, and gear five clockwise again. So the right side of gear five moves downwards. The rope unwinds, and the bucket goes down. Follow Riddle Time for more riddles.
