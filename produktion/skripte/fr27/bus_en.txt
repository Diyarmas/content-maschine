Kids solve this in seconds, adults fail. Here is a bus in London. It is driving either to the left or to the right. The front and the back look exactly the same. Which way is the bus going? Write left or right in the comments. [pause] Three. Two. One. [pause] The answer: To the right. You can't see a single door. So the doors are on the other side. In the UK, bus doors are on the left, toward the pavement. So you are looking at the bus's right side, and the front points to the right. Follow Riddle Time for more riddles.
