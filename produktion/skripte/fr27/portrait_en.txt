This one sentence confuses almost everyone. Lord Ashford stands in his manor in front of an old portrait. His butler asks who is in the painting. The lord replies: Brothers and sisters I have none. But this man's father is my father's son. Who is in the portrait? Write it in the comments. [pause] Three. Two. One. [pause] The answer: His son. My father's son, with no siblings, is the lord himself. So the lord is the father of the man in the painting. The portrait shows his son.
