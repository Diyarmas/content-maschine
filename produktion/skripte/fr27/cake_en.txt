One of these three stole the pie, and only one of them tells the truth. Can you find the thief? Margaret bakes a cherry pie and leaves it to cool by the open window. Ten minutes later, the plate is empty. Three neighbours are standing in the yard, Emma, Jack and Lily. Emma says, Jack did it. Jack says, I didn't do it. Lily says, I didn't do it either. Only one of them is telling the truth. Who stole the pie? Write your answer in the comments. [pause] Three. Two. One. [pause] The answer: Lily. If Emma told the truth, Jack did it, but then Lily would be telling the truth too. That's two truths, so Emma is lying, and Jack is innocent. That means Jack is the one telling the truth. So Lily is lying, and she took the pie. Follow Riddle Time for more riddles.
