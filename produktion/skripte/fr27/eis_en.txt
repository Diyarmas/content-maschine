One of these three is lying, and one glass gives the liar away. A scorching summer night. At ten o'clock, a pricey necklace vanishes from the Whitmore villa. Detective Miller arrives on the terrace shortly after. Three guests are sitting there with lemonade. Number one, Priya: I've been sitting out here for two hours. Number two, Jack: I went swimming and just got out of the pool. Number three, Oliver: I've also been sitting here for two hours, reading. Look closely at the glasses. Who is lying? Write one, two or three in the comments. [pause] Three. Two. One. [pause] The answer: Oliver, number three. His glass still has sharp-edged ice cubes. On a night this hot, they would have melted long ago, just like in Priya's glass. Oliver only just grabbed that drink. He was never on the terrace. Follow Riddle Time for more riddles.
