Only one answer is right, and almost everyone picks the wrong one. A power cut at Grandma Rose's house. She lights five candles of equal length, all at the same time. Then she blows them out one after another. When the power comes back, this is what you see. Five candles, all different lengths. Which candle did Grandma Rose blow out first? Write the number in the comments. [pause] Three. Two. One. [pause] The answer: The longest candle, number four. It burned for the shortest time. The earlier a candle goes out, the less wax melts away. Most people pick the shortest one, but that one burned the longest. Follow Riddle Time for more riddles.
