Whoever loses wins the whole farm. Old farmer Rajesh has two sons, Arjun and Rohan. Each of them gets a horse. Rajesh says: Whoever's horse finishes last inherits the farm. So both ride as slowly as they can. For days, they barely move. Then an old friend whispers two words to them. Instantly, both jump up and race off as fast as they can. What were the two words? Write them in the comments. [pause] Three. Two. One. [pause] The answer: Swap horses. Now each one rides the other's horse. Whoever crosses the line first makes sure his own horse finishes last.
