One cut too many, and the pay is ruined. Goldsmith Carter hires an apprentice, Sam, for exactly seven days. Sam's pay is a golden chain with seven links. Every evening, Sam wants exactly one link, no more and no less. But every cut through the gold costs Carter a fortune. Most people instantly say six cuts. But it works with far fewer. What is the smallest number of cuts Carter needs? Write your number in the comments. [pause] Three. Two. One. [pause] The answer: One single cut is enough. Carter cuts out the third link. Now he has three pieces with one, two and four links. On day one, he gives Sam the single link. On day two, he gives the two-link piece and takes the single link back. He keeps swapping every evening until Sam has all seven. Follow Riddle Time for more riddles.
