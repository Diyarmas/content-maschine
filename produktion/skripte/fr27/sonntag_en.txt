One of these four is lying, and the day of the week gives him away. Sunday morning, eight o'clock. A diamond vanishes from Lord Pemberton's study. Detective Miller questions the staff. The cook, Margaret: I was making breakfast. The gardener, George: I was watering the roses. The maid, Lucy: I was making the beds upstairs. The butler, Charles: I was fetching the post from the mailbox. Who is lying? Write the name in the comments. [pause] Three. Two. One. [pause] The answer: Charles, the butler. It is Sunday. There is no post delivered on a Sunday. So he wasn't at the mailbox, he was in the study. Follow Riddle Time for more riddles.
